Conflict Events in Relation to Mineral Infrastructure
Democratic Republic of the Congo, 2021–2026

This map looks at where conflict has taken place in relation to mineral sites and major roads across the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The three conflict categories show whether events happened near mineral infrastructure, near other major roads, or away from both. The aim is to make visible how unevenly conflict is distributed across these different parts of the country.

Sources: ACLED conflict-event data; USGS ScienceBase, Compilation of Geospatial Data for the Mineral Industries and Related Infrastructure of Africa; author’s spatial analysis and classification. Map by Fauwaz Khan, Columbia University, 2026.
